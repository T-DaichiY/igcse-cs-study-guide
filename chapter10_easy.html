10.1 Logic gates

the six gates
🍎 In plain words

A logic gate takes 1s and 0s in and gives one out. AND needs both 1; OR needs one; NOT flips; NAND/NOR/XOR are the twist versions.

The gates and their truth tables (NOT: 0→1, 1→0)
NAND, NOR and XOR → remember: a bubble on the output means NOT. XOR is 1 only when the inputs differ.

🔑 Key terms

Logic gate
A component that takes one or more binary inputs and produces one binary output.
NOT
Single-input gate: the output is the opposite of the input.
AND
Output is 1 only if both inputs are 1.
OR
Output is 1 if at least one input is 1.
NAND
NOT AND: output is 0 only if both inputs are 1.
NOR
NOT OR: output is 1 only if both inputs are 0.
XOR (EOR)
Exclusive OR: output is 1 only if the two inputs are different.
Truth table
Lists every possible combination of binary inputs and the output each produces.
Logic circuit
Gates connected to represent a problem; limited to three inputs and one output.
Logic expression
A written form of a circuit using gate names, e.g. X = (A AND B) OR C.
